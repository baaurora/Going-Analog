# 12.12 assets

Art for the 12.12 page. Most of it is now placed. The files still listed as
missing are asked for by the page already, and each one is quietly stood down
until it exists, so dropping the real file in here is the whole of the change.
No markup or CSS has to be touched when they arrive.

## On disk and in use

Sent 2026-11-08, second batch. All eight already have transparent
backgrounds, so they sit straight on the white page with no knockout work.

The four envelopes run as a stop motion in the hero, in the order
closed-2, closed-1, open-2, open-1. They share a canvas and, measured off
their alpha, they share a body: the mouth sits at 45.01% of the image in every
frame. A second copy of the body clipped to below that line is stacked in front
of the card, which is what lets the card genuinely slide out of a photograph.

`card-blank.webp` is the card that comes out. Its rectangle measures
1051 x 718 inside the 1400 square and is tilted 34.41 degrees about the centre,
so the page rotates it back by exactly that to stand it upright. Change the
file and those two numbers in 12.12/index.html have to be re-measured.

The two film scans are the backdrop. `photo-backyard.jpg` is the one in use.

| file | what it is | pixels |
|---|---|---|
| `doily-oval.png` | cream paper doily, oval, scalloped lace edge | 262 x 197 |
| `doily-rect.png` | cream paper doily, upright rectangle, lace border | 283 x 424 |
| `doily-square.png` | cream paper doily, square, lace border around a plain diamond | 296 x 312 |
| `envelope-open-1.webp` | pink vellum envelope, flap up, fills the frame | 1113 x 1413 |
| `envelope-open-2.webp` | pink vellum envelope, flap up, sits lower in frame | 1113 x 1413 |
| `envelope-closed-1.webp` | pink vellum envelope, flap down, shallow V | 1113 x 1413 |
| `envelope-closed-2.webp` | pink vellum envelope, flap down, deeper V | 1113 x 1413 |
| `card-blank.webp` | plain cream note card, blank, tilted | 1400 x 1400 |

The four envelopes are the same pink vellum envelope on the same canvas in
different states. Two open and two closed means they could be run as frames of
an opening, but Bianca has not said that, so do not build it on that guess.

The doilies are small files at their native size. The envelopes and the card
are big. Resize before putting any of them on the page, because a 474 KB
envelope is more than the whole rest of the site weighs.
