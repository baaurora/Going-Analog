Going Analog

GOING ANALOG is an organization based in the theory that good friendships and memories aren't born out of phones and algorithms, but rather, created in their absence.

In the increasingly digital age, where everything can be "optimized", from your fitness routine to shopping, we wanted to protect the most human of human things: connection and community.

Through intentional gatherings, where we take your phones away for a night, we provide a space to step away from your screens, dance the night away, and cultivate meaningful interactions, albeit sometimes awkward!

Yet, we encourage your participation and full buy-in to these events, because…the best memories don't involve your phone in hand.

How it works

  1. Drop your phone off in the phone jail (don't worry, we'll keep it safe!)
  2. Socialize IRL…Yes, in real life.
  3. Come again soon!
