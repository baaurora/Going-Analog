What this is

Going Analog is a collective. The phone goes in the box at the door and everything else follows from that.

House rules

  1. Phones go in the box. All of them. No “I'll just keep it in my pocket.”
  2. Bring a disposable if you have one. If you don't, we've got some.
  3. Talk to someone you didn't come with.
  4. $10-20 sliding scale. $100 if you work at an AI startup, I know you can afford it.
  5. Photos go up here about a week later. That's the only place they go.

The photos

Everything gets shot on film and disposables. The rolls go to a lab and come back about a week later, and whatever survives goes up here.

Nothing gets posted anywhere else. No stories, no tagging, no algorithm deciding who got to see you having a good time.
